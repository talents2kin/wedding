# 16 — Planner team members

**What to build:** A Pro or Agency Planner can invite Team Members to their Planner Account by email. Each Team Member is assigned a role (Admin / Editor / Viewer) that applies across all Weddings the Planner manages. The Planner can remove Team Members at any time, immediately revoking their access to all Weddings. This feature is locked on the Starter and Free tiers.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Planner Account + Wedding list.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Pro or Agency Planner can invite a Team Member by email with a chosen role (Admin / Editor / Viewer)
- [ ] The invited Team Member receives an email with an accept link
- [ ] Accepting the invite grants the Team Member access to all Weddings under that Planner Account with the assigned role
- [ ] Role permissions match those defined in T15 (Admin / Editor / Viewer) applied across all Weddings
- [ ] The Planner can change a Team Member's role
- [ ] The Planner can remove a Team Member, immediately revoking access to all Weddings
- [ ] The Planner Account settings page lists all Team Members and their roles
- [ ] On Starter and Free tiers, the team invite UI is locked with an upgrade prompt
