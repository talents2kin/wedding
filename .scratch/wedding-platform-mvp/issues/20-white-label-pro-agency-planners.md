# 20 — White-label for Pro/Agency Planners

**What to build:** A Pro or Agency Planner can upload their agency logo and configure a sender name (the name displayed to Guests on digital Invitations and Notifications). Invitations and Notifications sent for that Planner's Weddings display the agency branding in place of the platform branding. The platform logo is fully replaced — not shown alongside the agency logo. This feature is locked on Starter and Free tiers.

**Blocked by:** 08 — Invitation template catalog + digital delivery, 18 — Subscriptions — Stripe.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Pro or Agency Planner can upload an agency logo from their account settings
- [ ] A Pro or Agency Planner can configure a sender name (displayed on digital Invitations and Notifications)
- [ ] Invitations generated for that Planner's Weddings display the agency logo in place of the platform logo
- [ ] The platform logo does not appear alongside the agency logo — it is fully replaced
- [ ] The sender name on digital Invitations and Notifications reflects the configured agency name
- [ ] White-label settings apply to all Weddings under that Planner Account
- [ ] On Starter and Free tiers, the white-label settings UI shows an upgrade prompt
- [ ] A Wedding created by a Couple (not a Planner) never shows white-label controls
